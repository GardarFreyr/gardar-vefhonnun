What is included.

The final price depends on the scope of the project and is always confirmed before any work starts.

Quotes
The prices above are starting prices. I quote for your specific project and that price holds, unless you later ask for additions that were not part of the agreement.
Changes
Minor changes late in the process are included according to the scope of the project. A completely new design after a design has been approved counts as a new project.
Text
The client normally provides the final text unless we agree otherwise.
Photos
The client provides photographs of the business. You need to hold the rights to any images you send me.
Domain
You own your domain and pay its annual fee directly to the registrar. I help with the setup, but the domain itself is not included in the price.
Hosting
Sites are hosted with Netlify. Built the way I build them they fit inside Netlify's free tier, though a large project can require a paid tier.
Third party services
Domains, hosting and other third party services can be a separate cost depending on the project. I tell you in advance if any apply.
Maintenance
Maintenance is optional and costs 7,500 ISK a month. There is no lock in and it can be stopped at any time.
